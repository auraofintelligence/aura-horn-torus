What this is

An interactive demo of a horn torus lattice: 288 addressable cells on a surface that folds between a flat sheet and a closed torus, stacked seven shells deep inside a geosphere carrying an Earth map. Everything runs in one HTML page in the browser.

What it is for

It shows one component of a wider design. That design would map a person's own information into addressable space: interior faces private to the person, exterior faces shared on their terms, with maps of self, place and mind kept close together for intuitive navigation. This page demonstrates the surface, the folding and the addressing, and claims nothing beyond that.

Provenance

The interaction pattern was sparked by Interactive Photo Cube by Amit Asulin on brik.space. The geometry, the morph and all code here are original. The horn torus mind-mapping design predates that reference by roughly a decade.
