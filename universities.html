Coverage, sources and how to contribute

This directory covers active education organisations in the ROR v2.11 snapshot of 3 August 2026. ROR covers research organisations: these records include universities, colleges and other education bodies. It is not a register of accreditation or a complete count of every university on Earth.

The older university list remains traceable in separate globe layers. Unresolved historical identities stay in the review ledger. The wider ROR layer adds current registry identities independently, including institutions that were missing from that older list.

ROR metadata is CC0; its GeoNames locality data is CC BY 4.0. Membership evidence has its own source and date. Registry relationships describe organisational links, not assumed collaboration or endorsement.

Appearing here does not establish GAJRA Earth membership. Read the public invitation. An individual's signature does not speak for their institution.
