About this directory

This is the page list from the Aura workbook, laid out as the app would carry it: every section, every group, every page and every item, each one addressable. Icons come from the original prototype screens where a screen existed, and fall back to a plain glyph where one did not. Tap Edit icons and then any icon to swap it for another from the library, upload your own, or copy a prompt and generate one. Your choices live in this browser only. There is no account and nothing is uploaded.
