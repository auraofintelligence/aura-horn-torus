About this directory and its sources

This directory combines all current entries from DFAT's Missions in Australia and Consulates in Australia indexes. It includes embassies, high commissions, consulates, consulates-general, other diplomatic posts and honorary posts where DFAT lists them.

Moved offices, uncertain identities and missions accredited from another country need different treatment. A non-resident mission is not placed in Canberra simply because it is accredited to Australia. Postal boxes and personal residences are not substitutes for public office locations.

Each mapped record includes its coordinate source and basis. An office or building position is not a guaranteed visitor entrance. A record marked Not mapped may have a verified address but still lack sufficient coordinate evidence.
